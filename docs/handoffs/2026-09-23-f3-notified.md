## What changed

| Piece | Change |
|---|---|
| Migration 0005 | `ALTER TABLE "reward_decisions" ADD COLUMN "notified_at" timestamp with time zone;`. Additive and nullable, with no backfill: `reward_decisions` doesn't exist on Neon until 0004 is applied in the same step. |
| `notifyReward` | A job with a `decisionId` is skipped if that decision is already marked. After Telegram accepts the message, the job marks it (`markNotified`, guarded by `notified_at IS NULL`). |
| `evaluateReward` | A completed decision's message job carries its `decisionId`. Pending-evidence and reconciliation messages are unchanged and carry no marker. |
| Sweep part (c) | `strandedWork` returns decisions with `notified_at` null whose `acceptedAt` (the completion time under the lock) is past the 10-minute grace and within `NOTIFY_WINDOW_MS` (24 h). `recoverRewardWork` rebuilds the message from the stored decision and queues it. |

Delivery is at least once. A crash between Telegram accepting the message and the mark being written sends the message again. A message Telegram still refuses 24 hours after the decision is abandoned. The decision itself is never affected.
