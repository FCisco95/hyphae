# Candidate 1.3.0 policy scenarios

These are synthetic review scenarios derived from the founder-approved rules, not real posts or founder-labelled numeric grades. They describe the behavior to evaluate with the model; schema/unit tests cannot establish that the model follows them. They are not platform or legal clearance.

For each case, supply the complete target post and contribution. Do not reconstruct missing target posts from the coaching screenshots.

| Scenario | Expected treatment |
|---|---|
| A release announces public payout receipts. A member explains how those receipts help check distributions. | Eligible for quality credit based on specificity and usefulness. |
| The same release receives a reasoned criticism that receipts alone do not explain rejected contributions, with a request for the missing reasoning. | Same opportunity to earn as praise. No `off_topic` or `guideline_breach` solely for criticism. |
| A reply only says "great project" or "lfg" and claims it received many likes. | The popularity claim does not raise quality; generic applause remains low effort. |
| A member says "I hold MYCEL" and explains a specific product decision they value. | The truthful first-person holding statement is not a buy/hold instruction. Grade the actual explanation. |
| A member says only "I hold MYCEL". | Ownership is not a breach, but it does not establish a useful contribution or guarantee points. |
| A target supplies relevant financial facts; a reply explains an uncertain valuation scenario with its assumptions. | No automatic breach for price discussion; assess the supporting reasoning and context. |
| "We are going up to 100M easy" or "Could hit 100M, NFA" with no supporting basis. | Unsupported hype remains a guideline breach; hedging alone is not grounding. |
| A useful explanation includes an honest statement that the author is eligible for community contribution rewards. | Grade the explanation; the disclosure itself is not low effort, AI slop, or disqualifying promotion. Campaign eligibility is a separate question. |
| A member repeats a project name or asks a question already answered in the target. | Do not award specificity for name-dropping or usefulness merely for asking a question. |

Use paired praise/criticism examples with comparable specificity and effort to check sentiment bias; this does not require identical scores for different text. Compare the reasoning as well as the flags. Confirm acceptable numeric ranges with the founder before importing cases into the numeric evaluation harness.
