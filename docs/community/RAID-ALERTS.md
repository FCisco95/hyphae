# Raid alerts

In a registered community's group, /alerts offers a private bot link for that community. Open it and choose whether to receive private raid alerts. The bot verifies current group membership before subscribing or delivering. A forwarded link does not grant membership or access.

Members control their own subscription. A blocked bot or inaccessible private chat can prevent delivery; delivery is not proof that a member saw the raid. Turning alerts off does not change contribution history, points or payout eligibility.

The designated community admin manages raids. Alert subscription, group administration and a linked wallet grant no additional operator permission. Public raid state is available through the [read API](https://hyphae-api.fly.dev/docs).

Source: [alert commands](../../apps/api/src/bot/commands/notifications.ts), [raid lifecycle](../../apps/api/src/bot/commands/raid-lifecycle.ts). No Telegram ID or subscription roster belongs in public screenshots.
