# Try the live pilot

1. Open the [public epoch-2 audit](https://hyphae-delta.vercel.app/c/HudkzEWpcUnTYFZMMcbNdwk1S5Am26J2SyEh4NfFworg/e/2). Its settlement panel and four paid rows link to mainnet claim receipts.
2. Open the [wallet record](https://hyphae-delta.vercel.app/wallet/MAoRn1JFD66gcXHiCqcAgppXBCR4TP8pKT4XV7HVhAB) and compare it with the [finalized receipt index](evidence/2026-10-10-mainnet-payout.md).
3. For participation, join the registered community and use its official bot's menu. Link your own wallet with a readable message signature, complete the private rules checklist, and submit work to an open raid.
4. Use /me in the group to open your private checklist. Rules-test and hold outcomes are not posted publicly. [Member receipts](community/MEMBER-RECEIPTS.md) explain provisional points, frozen decisions and issue reports.

Points do not promise an allocation. A claim requires a leaf in a published epoch; a wallet without a leaf cannot claim. Already paid leaves cannot be claimed again. A waiting wallet approval can be cancelled; check the wallet and payment state before deliberately retrying.

The scorer reads text, not images or video. Report a disputed score with the contribution link and a concrete reason. Corrections preserve the old decision; post-close corrections do not change a frozen payout. For security issues, use [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/FCisco95/hyphae-program/security/advisories/new).

For local development and checks, see [README](../README.md#develop). No production credential or payment is needed to build and test the code.
