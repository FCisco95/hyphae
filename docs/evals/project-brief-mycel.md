Organic is a platform that acts as a community hub for communities. It builds tools that help communities reach higher milestones and last longer than a few days, and it lets communities share rewards with their members through the Hyphae protocol.

MYCEL ($MYCEL) is a community token on Solana created by Cisco (@FCisco95) for Organic. MYCEL was first the name of Organic's AI layer; the token was created as a test, and the community liked it.

Hyphae is a standalone product, built for the Colosseum hackathon: a public, wallet-bound record of members' contributions, scored against a published rubric, with rewards paid from a vault. Cisco published an article about it on 2026-09-21 titled "Your wallet knows what you bought. Hyphae shows what you built."

Known project facts:
- 10% of the supply is locked for 2 years (announced Aug 28).
- On day 1 there was no product, only the token, trading at about 50k market cap.
- Only holders' organic engagement is scored; blind shilling is not rewarded.
- Scoring results are published on-chain.
- Contribution guidelines are to be published when the system is set.
- Cisco welcomes other builders and is open to negotiating revenue with them.
