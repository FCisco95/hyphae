Link your own wallet

Your wallet will ask you to sign a readable message. It is free and moves no funds. A later claim signs a transaction.

Check the signing page's exact domain: .

Use only your own private link from the official bot. Never open a forwarded wallet link, forward this link, or send it to support. Cancel any transfer, approval or seed-phrase request.

Choose a wallet in this browser

On a phone?

  1. Copy your ORIGINAL private bot URL below, including its fragment.
  2. Paste it into your installed wallet app's own browser. A mobile system browser may not expose the wallet.
  3. Select a detected wallet, sign the unchanged free message, then return to your own group /me and confirm the same wallet.


The page removes the private fragment from the address bar. Do not copy that stripped address. A reload loses the in-memory link: close the previous signing page, then open the ORIGINAL bot URL or get a fresh /link in your registered group. Links expire after fifteen minutes and are single-use.
